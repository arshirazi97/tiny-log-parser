Why each is here:

  rule_parser.py        an arm, and the system the new corpus must judge
                        independently. Frozen so it cannot be quietly improved
                        after seeing which lines it fails.
  build_train_real.py   its normalisation layer is the training LABEL function.
                        Frozen because that layer is what the coupling finding
                        is about.
  schema_v2.py          holds SPEC_EVAL, the text both LLM arms receive.
                        Frozen so neither arm's prompt drifts mid-experiment.
  score_arms.py         the metric definitions. Frozen so "exact" and
                        "six-field" cannot be redefined after the fact.
  ADJUDICATION.md       the labelling rules. Frozen because the P1 corpus is
                        labelled against them and they must not move to fit
                        what labelling turns up.
  generate_v2.py        the synthetic renderers. Frozen as the v2 baseline;
                        the P2 generator additions land in a NEW file.
